# Lab 25 — AI Review Policy

## Must reject
Any AI output that invents data, fabricates customers, alters fixtures, or proposes behavior outside the allowed design scope. Reject anything that contradicts domain rules, introduces new fields, or rewrites business logic.

## Must check
Confirm the AI’s answer matches the established fixtures, follows the URI and method/status conventions, respects validation rules, and stays within the lab’s boundaries. Check for hallucinations, missing constraints, or silent changes to the contract.

## Where to record review
Record the review directly in the notes file for the exercise, under the AI‑Review section, so the reasoning and corrections are visible alongside the deliverables.

## Scope
Pre-lab only.
